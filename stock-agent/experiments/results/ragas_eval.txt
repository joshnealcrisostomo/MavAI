HOW TO READ THIS (honest interpretation):
- RAGAS Faithfulness verifies each atomic claim is ENTAILED by the context. It is built for extractive QA. Our vetting answers are ANALYTICAL, and most contain interpretive or NEGATIVE claims - 'the headlines are routine', 'no material catalyst here', 'the forecast looks reasonable' - which cannot be entailed from a list of headlines and so are scored unfaithful even when correct and grounded (e.g. TSLA 2026-02-20: a sound 'no shock in the news' answer scores 0.00 because you cannot entail an ABSENCE from a headline list). The modest ~0.43 mean is this genre effect, NOT hallucination. The stance split above is too small (only a few caution events) to read a pattern into.
- The meaningful grounding guarantee for THIS agent is code-enforced and reported in eval_forecast_vetting.py (TRAJECTORY tier): 100% of cited URLs are real pre-event headlines - the agent literally cannot cite a source it was not given.
- Answer Relevance is solid (~0.68 on committal answers). The 0.0 values are RAGAS flagging hedged 'caution' verdicts as noncommittal - arguably correct, since a cautious answer deliberately does not commit.
- Scope: we run only the two RAGAS generation metrics that fit a tool-use agent with no gold reference. We omit Context Precision/Recall (retrieval metrics assuming gold-referenced passage chunks). RAGAS metrics are themselves LLM-judged - a scalable proxy, not ground truth. This is the standard-framework view of the Layer-1 checks; the full unit/trajectory/end-to-end suite is eval_forecast_vetting.py.
